Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. App and local game data

Guess to Survive (com.zemvuu.guesstosurvive) is an Android word game. It saves game progress, diamonds, unlocked scenes and themes, language and gameplay preferences locally in your device's WebView storage. The app does not require an account. Game progress is saved on your device.

2. Advertising

Google AdMob provides banner, rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Depending on your location, consent choices and Google settings, its SDK may process device and advertising identifiers, network information, approximate location inferred from IP address, interactions with ads and diagnostic information for serving and measuring ads, security and fraud prevention. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy information.

3. Privacy choices

The game integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request advertising choices where required. You can review available controls using Settings → Privacy options. Rejecting optional consent does not prevent gameplay; some ads may remain available where legally permitted.

4. Analytics

The app includes Firebase Analytics, with analytics collection disabled by default. Game progress is not synchronized to a cloud database. Read Firebase's privacy information.

5. In-app purchases

If you purchase diamonds or remove ads through Google Play, Google processes your payment. The app may process your purchase status, purchase tokens and entitlements to deliver or restore purchases. We do not receive complete payment card details.

6. Website hosting

This website does not use its own tracking scripts or account registration. The GitHub Pages hosting provider may process technical logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local progress remains until app data is cleared or the app is removed, subject to device settings and backups. You may erase local game data through Android's app storage settings; doing so also removes progress. Google services follow their own retention and privacy policies.

8. Children and updates

The game is not specifically directed at children. This policy will be updated if the app's features, integrations or target audience change.

9. Questions

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact information on the Google Play listing or through the developer profile.
