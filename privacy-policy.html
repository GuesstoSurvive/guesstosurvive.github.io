Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Pre-release: This policy describes the known current test build and will be reviewed against the final Google Play release before publication.

1. App and local game data

Guess to Survive (com.zemvuu.guesstosurvive) is an Android word game. It saves game progress, diamonds, unlocked scenes and themes, language and gameplay preferences locally in your device's WebView storage. The current version does not offer an online account or cloud save synchronization.

2. Advertising

Google AdMob provides banner, rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Depending on your location, consent choices and Google settings, its SDK may process device and advertising identifiers, network information, approximate location inferred from IP address, interactions with ads and diagnostic information for serving and measuring ads, security and fraud prevention. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy information.

3. Privacy choices

The game integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request advertising choices where required. You can review available controls using Settings → Privacy options. Rejecting optional consent does not prevent gameplay; some ads may remain available where legally permitted.

4. Analytics

The Firebase Analytics SDK is included but analytics collection is disabled by default pending completion and verification of consent handling. There is currently no cloud save database. Any future activation will be reflected here and in the app's privacy controls. Read Firebase's privacy information.

5. In-app purchases

Google Play Billing is planned for optional diamonds and ad removal. The current development store uses simulations, not real payments. When activated, Google Play will process payments and the app may handle purchase tokens or entitlement state; we will not receive complete payment card data.

6. Website hosting

This website does not use its own tracking scripts or account registration. The GitHub Pages hosting provider may process technical logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local progress remains until app data is cleared or the app is removed, subject to device settings and backups. You may erase local game data through Android's app storage settings; doing so also removes progress. Google services follow their own retention and privacy policies.

8. Children and updates

The game is not specifically directed at children. This policy will be updated if the app's features, integrations or target audience change.

9. Questions

Contact the developer using the contact details on the Google Play listing when published.

Publication checklist: verify developer contact method, final SDK behavior and the Google Play Data safety form before release.
